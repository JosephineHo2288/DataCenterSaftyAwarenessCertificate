> 
> - **A)** Removing up to four adjacent tiles in a line to facilitate long cable pulls.
> - **B)** Removing a maximum of two adjacent floor tiles at one time in a single area.
> - **C)** Transporting standard pallet jacks over perforated airflow tiles without distribution plates.
> - **D)** Removing floor tiles without management authorization, provided the work takes under 5 minutes.
> 
> <details>
> <summary><b>Click to view answer key & rationale</b></summary>
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **B**  
> **Rationale:** Standard safety protocol mandates that no more than two adjacent tiles be removed simultaneously without temporary bracing to preserve grid stability, protect pressurized plenum airflow, and minimize personal fall hazards.
> </details>

## 1. Hazard Profile & Injury Prevention

Same-level slip, trip, and fall hazards cause significant musculoskeletal injuries in high-density computer rooms.

* **Primary Danger Zones:** Unseated floor tiles, temporary patch cords, HVAC condensation, fluid leaks, and staged packaging.
* **Common Physical Injuries:** Sprains, strains, contusions, and fractures targeting knees, ankles, wrists, elbows, hips, and the lumbar spine.
* **Mandatory PPE:** Slip-resistant, closed-toe safety footwear is required for all data hall access.

---

## 2. Raised Floor System Protocols

The under-floor space (plenum) houses critical air pathways, HVAC piping, and power/fiber routing. Mishandling floor systems compromises both personnel safety and facility uptime.

* **Authorization & Tools:** Only authorized personnel may lift floor tiles. Always use matched suction-cup or mechanical hook tile pullers.
* **Removal Constraints:** 
  * Never pull more than **two adjacent tiles** at once without structural reinforcement.
  * Barricade open plenum sections immediately using high-visibility safety cones or physical barriers.
* **Load Limits:**
  * Solid tiles and perforated (vented) tiles have distinct static and dynamic weight ratings.
  * Standard pallet jacks and excessive rolling loads are strictly prohibited on raised grids without load-distribution plywood or plates.
  * Calculate total server rack payload prior to populating cabinets.

---

## 3. Walkway & Environmental Housekeeping

* **Zero-Tolerance Staging:** Server aisles and egress routes must never be used for temporary box, pallet, or tool storage.
* **Cable Containment:** Route all interim cabling through overhead cable trays or dedicated sub-floor channels—never across open footpaths.
* **Signage & Elevation:** Deploy high-visibility *Wet Floor* signs immediately during fluid cleanup, and mark floor elevation transitions (ramps, curbs) with high-contrast safety tape.
